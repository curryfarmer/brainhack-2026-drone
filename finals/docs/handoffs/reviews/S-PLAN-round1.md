## BUG / CONV
finals/mission/planning/visibility_graph.py:227: LOW BUG: _polyline_to_legs subdivision splits by the caller-supplied max_leg_cm, which has NO relation to inflation_m, yet the module docstring (lines 8-9, 35-36) and the function claim it "bounds per-move open-loop drift under the inflation margin". A caller passing a large max_leg_cm with a small inflation_m gets legs whose accumulated open-loop drift can exceed the margin and clip the real crate, with no loud refusal. Fix: either derive/clamp max_leg_cm from inflation_m and a drift-per-cm budget, or downgrade the docstring claim to "bounds drift to the CALLER-chosen sub-leg length" so the guarantee is not overstated (NAV-5 owns the budget link).
finals/mission/planning/polygon_tools.py:120: LOW BUG: inflate_polygon's miter uses sin_half = max(abs(sin_half), _MIN_SIN_HALF_ANGLE) with abs() and the sum-of-normals direction; for a REFLEX (non-convex) vertex the miter vector points the wrong way and the corner over/under-shoots (docstring acknowledges over-shoot only). Safe for the convex crate footprints actually used (inflation only enlarges), but NAV-2 does NOT enforce convexity, so a hand-authored concave keep-out could inflate to a self-intersecting ring the planner then mis-tests. Fix: assert convexity at NAV-2 (or document that concave keep-outs are unsupported by inflate_polygon and reject them), not a code change here.
finals/mission/planning/polygon_tools.py:241: LOW BUG: segment_enters_polygon relies on the midpoint-inside fallback to catch a segment that enters the interior with NO proper edge crossing (vertex-to-vertex chord); for a sufficiently concave polygon a vertex-to-vertex interior chord can have its midpoint OUTSIDE the polygon and slip through as "does not enter". Not reachable with convex inflated crates (midpoint of an interior chord is interior). Fix: none required for convex inputs; if concave keep-outs are ever allowed, test edge-entry by sampling more than the midpoint or by counting interior sub-spans.
finals/mission/planning/visibility_graph.py:220: LOW CONV: _reconstruct's `while current in came_from` has no explicit deadline/iteration-bound reference (convention 3). It is provably bounded (came_from is an acyclic tree, chain <= n), but every other while in the slice carries a bound comment. Fix: add a one-line "bounded: came_from is an acyclic predecessor tree, len <= n" comment for parity.
